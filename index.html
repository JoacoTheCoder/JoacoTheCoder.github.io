About

I studied marketing and business analytics, went straight into fund data, and found out the interesting part happens before the report.

Day to day that means SQL Server, Excel and Morningstar data — building reports, maintaining databases, answering questions about how funds are actually doing. The projects here are the same instinct pointed at things I was curious about on my own time.

What I'm looking for is more of the part I keep gravitating toward: getting data into a state where the question becomes answerable, and then making the answer legible to someone who wasn't in the weeds with you.
